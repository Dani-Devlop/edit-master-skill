# voice.md — Brand Voice Template

Copy this file to your agent's config location (for example
`~/.claude/edit-master/voice.md`), fill it in, and tell the skill where it
lives. Every phase reads it. Ten minutes here is worth more than any prompt
you will ever write: a script in the wrong voice is unreadable on camera, and
a caption in the wrong voice reads like a brand.

Delete the guidance lines as you fill each section in.

## Who I am talking to

One person. Name them properly — not "creators", but "someone with under a
thousand followers who posts twice a month and cannot work out why nothing
breaks 400 views". Platform, what they want, what they are afraid of.

## How I actually talk

Paste the transcript of your best-performing video here, or three of them.
Not what you wish you sounded like — the words you actually use, with the
fillers and the tics.

## Words I never use

The ones that are not mine. Be specific: "unlock", "game-changer", "dive in",
"let's get into it", "in today's video". The skill refuses these for you.

## Words I do use

Your tics. The phrases that are yours. Keep them in the scripts.

## What I will not claim

Numbers you cannot show, results that are not yours, tools you have not used.
Write them down so the skill refuses them instead of you catching it in the
edit.
