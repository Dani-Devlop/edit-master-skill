# Example 1 — Onboarding answers + edit plan (short-form vertical, minimal style)

This is what a filled onboarding and Phase 1-4 output looks like.

## Onboarding (the 10 questions, answered)

1. Main platform: **Instagram**
2. Video type: **Talking head**
3. Target length: **Under 60s (target 30s)**
4. Brand style: **Minimal**
5. Main goal: **Grow audience**
6. Available tools: **Claude + CapCut**
7. Budget per video: **Free**
8. Delivery speed: **Same day**
9. Footage source: **Already recorded** (one 4-min raw take, 1080x1920)
10. Brand assets: **Colors (#111 background, #FAFAFA text, #FF4D00 accent) + fonts (Inter)**

voice.md exists. One true specific from the creator: they cut proposal
turnaround from 5 hours to 20 minutes with one template.

## Phase 3 — hooks (scored by hand on the 5-property panel)

| formula | spoken line | on screen | S | A | St | C | B | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| #5 Time Collapse | "Proposals used to take me five hours. Twenty minutes now." | 5 HOURS -> 20 MIN | 88 | 71 | 58 | 64 | 100 | **80 STRONG** |
| #1 Cost Confession | "I billed four hours a week for formatting. For two years." | 2 YEARS WASTED | 84 | 60 | 72 | 55 | 92 | 74 STRONG |
| #20 Permission | "You don't need a proposal tool to close clients." | YOU DON'T NEED A TOOL | 55 | 70 | 48 | 61 | 100 | 63 WORKABLE |

Winner: #5 Time Collapse. Ratio believable, reads in one glance, number is the
creator's own. Weakest property on the winner: STAKES — fixed in the stake
beat ("while you format, the client is signing someone else").

## Phase 4 — beat sheet (165 wpm)

```
B01 0:00.0-0:02.2  HOOK    spoken: "Proposals used to take me five hours."
                           on screen: 5 HOURS -> 20 MIN   (mid-action cold open)
B02 0:02.2-0:04.0  HOOK2   spoken: "Twenty minutes now."
                           on screen: 20 MIN
B03 0:04.0-0:07.5  STAKE   spoken: "While you're formatting, your client is
                           signing someone else's proposal."
B04 0:07.5-0:12.0  BODY    spoken: setup of the one template; screen recording
                           starts, cursor visible
B05 0:12.0-0:16.0  BODY    the template's three fields; zoom 110% on fields
B06 0:16.0-0:21.0  BODY    proof: sent-folder montage, timestamps visible
B07 0:21.0-0:25.5  PAYOFF  spoken: "That's the template. It's linked in bio."
                           on screen: STEAL THIS
B08 0:25.5-0:28.5  ASK     spoken: "Comment PROPOSAL and I'll send it."
B09 0:28.5-0:30.0  LOOP    spoken: "...five hours." (echo of B01 opening word)
```

Flags check: hook 2.2s (pass, <= 3s), no beat over 4.5s, every beat has
something concrete on screen, loop present.

## Phase 5 — render + QA

- ffmpeg cut list from B01-B09 (source take trimmed, filler "um"s cut)
- captions burned word-synced, Inter Bold, centered block at y=1180..1420
  (inside safe zone: above the y=1440 lower bound, right 230px clear)
- music: minimal electronic, -14 LUFS; SFX pop on B02 and B07
- grade: exposure matched across cuts, slight contrast curve, #111/#FAFAFA/#FF4D00
  used in text cards only
- humanizer: 3 artefacts stripped ("exciting", em dash, curly quotes) -> PASS 78
- caption (Job A): "Comment PROPOSAL and I'll send you the template." (54/125
  chars visible), one ask, 3 hashtags (#proposal #agency #freelance)

Delivery receipt ends with: **Ship it, or change it?**
