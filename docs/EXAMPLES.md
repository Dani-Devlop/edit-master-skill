# Worked Examples

Three complete runs, end to end: onboarding (all 10 questions), the
voice summary, the scored hook panel, the winning hook, the beat sheet,
the EDL, the caption file, and the 16-point QA report. All scenarios,
creators and numbers in this document are original to it.

Two notes before the examples:

- Phase 3 asks for three hook options on a short-form run (five on
  long-form). Each example below scores five formulas so the full 60/40
  verdict math is visible; the top two are still shown and the winner
  named, exactly as Phase 3 requires.
- Honest limits apply to every score here: these panels separate
  deliberately bad hooks from real ones well, and separate a creator's
  own hits from their own misses barely at all. A low score is a reason
  to look again; a high score is not a promise.

# Example 1 — 30s vertical talking-head reel (minimal style, TikTok)

## The brief

Mara, a freelance brand designer, wants a 30-second TikTok about the one
system that stopped revision creep. One true specific from her: free
revision rounds cost her $4,300 last year; the fix is a one-page brief
with three fields — scope, rounds, price — and round three is billed at
$450.

## Onboarding (the 10 questions, answered)

1. Main platform: **TikTok**
2. Video type: **Talking head**
3. Target length: **Under 60s (target 30s)**
4. Brand style: **Minimal**
5. Main goal: **Make money**
6. Available tools: **Claude + CapCut**
7. Budget per video: **Free**
8. Delivery speed: **Same day**
9. Footage source: **Already recorded** (one 4m12s raw take, 1080x1920)
10. Brand assets: **Colors (#0E0E0E background, #F5F1EA text, #2E6E5C
    accent) + font (Inter)**

## voice.md (summary)

- Talking to: freelance designers with under 2,000 followers who quote
  hourly and keep eating revision rounds.
- How I talk: short declaratives, contractions, "rounds", "one-pager",
  "four-fifty".
- Words I never use: unlock, game-changer, dive in, "in today's video".
- Words I do use: "rounds", "scope", "the brief prices it out".
- What I will not claim: client names, revenue figures, follower counts.
- Format: TikTok talking head under 60s, minimal; #0E0E0E / #F5F1EA /
  #2E6E5C on cards only; Inter; captions Inter SemiBold sentence case in
  the y=1180..1420 block; music lo-fi minimal, no trap drops; the one ask
  is a comment keyword.

Verdict math (60% mean + 40% weakest property):

```
#10: 0.6 x 79.2 + 0.4 x 70 (CURIOSITY)  = 47.52 + 28.00 = 75.52 -> 76 STRONG
#1:  0.6 x 75.0 + 0.4 x 55 (ADDRESS)    = 45.00 + 22.00 = 67.00 -> 67
#2:  0.6 x 72.8 + 0.4 x 54 (CURIOSITY)  = 43.68 + 21.60 = 65.28 -> 65
#20: 0.6 x 68.0 + 0.4 x 48 (STAKES)     = 40.80 + 19.20 = 60.00 -> 60
#11: 0.6 x 71.2 + 0.4 x 58 (STAKES)     = 42.72 + 23.20 = 65.92 -> 66
```

## Winning hook, and why

**#10 If This, Then Watch, 76 STRONG (weakest: CURIOSITY 70).** "Your
client" names the viewer inside six words; "round nine" is a number only
a designer's client work produces; the cost (unpaid rounds) is delivered
by the stake beat. #1 Cost Confession carries the biggest number but its
weakest property (ADDRESS 55) is the one short-form cannot hide: no
"you", no entry. Top two handed over per Phase 3: #10 (76) and #1 (67),
winner named.

   recording)
